## S1a: time fields
**International (run 1, 1,200 + 1,200):** `endDate` is a date-time, but in most strata one clock time dominates and several look like Eastern-time date-only values (e.g. 04:00/05:00 UTC = midnight US Eastern, 03:59/04:59 UTC = 23:59 Eastern); `startDate`/`events[].startDate` is within 60 minutes of a creation-like field for 86–100 % of markets (a listing time). Only tennis `events[].startTime` was recommended (158 of 159 markets, median start→resolution 1.8 h). (Run 2's international section was not captured in the logs.)

**US (run 2, 2,400 markets, per distinct event):**
- `startDate`: UNRELIABLE (listing time) in every stratum with data (60–100 % within 60 minutes of creation).
- `endDate`: only American football `RECOMMEND (human review)` (266 events, 92 % usable, top clock time 04:00:00 holds 32 %, 6 % Eastern placeholders); politics, basketball, hockey, combat and other sports fail the usable-share or clock-time rules; most other strata INSUFFICIENT_DATA (< 30 events).
- **`gameStartTime` is present on 100 % of sampled sports markets, including FUTURES markets, and looks like genuine kick-off times for single-game markets:** per distinct event the times of day are varied and plausible (American football 28 % at 18:00 UTC, i.e. 1 pm Eastern, 30 % at 20:00 Eastern; basketball 23 % at 01:00 UTC = 8 pm Eastern; hockey 40 % at 00:00 UTC = 7 pm Eastern; 17–29 distinct times per sport). Reach (≥ 100 markets from ≥ 30 events): American football, basketball, combat, hockey, other sport yes; baseball (9 events), soccer (8), tennis (2), golf, motorsport no.
- **Limitation found:** the slot-1 verdict printed `startDate` (rejected) and did not judge `gameStartTime` as a candidate for the same slot, so the verdict table understates `gameStartTime`. To be fixed in 4.0c.
- Events: 1,032 (international) vs 1,446 (US) in the samples; matched by participant names and date: **0** (the samples are not designed to overlap; D78 aliases not applied).
- US documentation (official) shows events filters **`live`** and **`ended`** for sports events (an in-play indicator candidate) and a sports API by sport and league: not yet used.
