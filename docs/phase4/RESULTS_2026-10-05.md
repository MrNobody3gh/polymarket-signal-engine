# Phase 4.0e — are the US exchange's `gameStartTime` values at 00:00:00Z placeholders or real evening starts? (production run, 5 Oct 2026)

Run: Railway region sfo, public unauthenticated endpoints, **no database key**, commit `9fb9100`; 272 requests; stopped at the sample caps (33,707 markets, 12,000 distinct events: **open listing only**; the sample has
**no resolved events**). Bundle checksum verified with the new `phase4:bundle-decode`. Evidence committed: `data/v_polymarket_us_gamestart.json`, `data/S1e_GAMESTART.md`. **No audit rule or threshold was changed (D68).**
6,065 events fall under daylight saving time (EDT) and about 5,935 under winter time (EST, from 1 Nov 2026); 1,837 events are at exactly 00:00:00Z.

## What the pre-registered rules said (as written before the counts)
Part A verdicts: sports:basketball **LOCAL_TIME_SHIFT**; sports:hockey and sports:other_sport **FIXED_UTC_PLACEHOLDER**; sports:american_football **UNDETERMINED**; soccer, combat, politics NO_MIDNIGHT_CLUSTER; tennis and baseball INSUFFICIENT_AFTER.
Part B: the 00:00Z group is **0 LIKELY_REAL / 298 LIKELY_PLACEHOLDER / 1,539 UNDETERMINED**, because the evidence that could corroborate is absent for them: the sports schedule has a start for only 12 of the 1,837 midnight events (0.7 %) and there are no resolved events in the sample.
**The contrast group (other clock times) is corroborated: the schedule start agrees within 15 minutes for 160 of the 161 events that have one (99.4 %)** (schedule coverage 161 of 1,832 events).
Part C: under the unchanged thresholds **no stratum passes slot 1 with `gameStartTime`**, even with corroborated events exempted (none are corroborated), and some strata fail rules the exemption does not touch: `startBeforeClose` (basketball, combat, politics), `topClock` (american_football, politics), `placeholderShare`.
Futures-type markets are placed at one instant: for example 234 american_football FUTURES events (2,823 markets) at one 00:00Z instant under EDT.

## A limit of the pre-registered Part A rule, and an AFTER-THE-FACT check (exploratory; not part of the pre-registration)
The rule tests only whether the 00:00Z cluster moves to 01:00Z. A 7 pm local start is 23:00Z under EDT and **00:00Z under EST**, so a genuine local-time cluster can *arrive at* 00:00Z after the change and look "fixed" (hockey: 00:00Z 587 events under EDT, 1,244 under EST, while 23:00Z falls from 533 to 301).
So I compared each sport's **whole clock-time distribution** before and after the change: the overlap of the EST distribution with the EDT distribution unshifted versus shifted by +1 hour (1.0 = identical):

| sport | events EDT / EST | overlap, no shift | overlap, +1 h | reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| basketball | 679 / 712 | 0.51 | **0.90** | shifts with daylight saving: local time |
| hockey | 665 / 485 | 0.32 | **0.91** | shifts with daylight saving: local time |
| combat | 90 / 95 | 0.03 | 0.50 | shifts |
| soccer | 169 / 87 | 0.20 | 0.46 | shifts |
| american_football | 250 / 172 | 0.05 | 0.14 | mostly futures at one instant; weak evidence either way |
| other_sport | 592 / 2,876 | 0.49 | 0.44 | **does not clearly shift**: a mixture; 845 moneyline events sit at 00:00Z under EST |
| tennis, baseball, esports, cricket, golf | fewer than 30 events on one side | n/m | n/m | cannot conclude |
**Reading (labelled as exploratory):** for basketball and hockey (and probably combat and soccer) the `gameStartTime` values, including those at 00:00Z, behave like **local start times**, so the audit's `MIDNIGHT_UTC` placeholder rule is probably rejecting genuine evening starts there. For "other_sport" a large 00:00Z cluster looks like a fixed-UTC placeholder. Futures-type markets (one instant for hundreds of events) are placeholders everywhere.

## What this does and does not establish
Established: the non-midnight `gameStartTime` values agree with the schedule where the schedule has a start (160 of 161); basketball and hockey times shift with daylight saving; futures markets share one instant.
**Not established:** that 00:00Z events are genuine (no schedule start for 99.3 % of them and no resolved events); whether `gameStartTime` would pass the audit even if exempted (other rules fail: `startBeforeClose`, `topClock`); the sample is open markets only; the after-the-fact table was not pre-registered, and sports with few post-transition events cannot be judged.
A side effect to follow up: `startBeforeClose` fails in basketball, combat and politics, which suggests the close field (`endDate`, a date-level value at about midnight Eastern) can precede the game start on the same day; that bears on the earlier slot-2 recommendation of `endDate` for american_football and combat, which should be re-examined per event.

## Options for the owner (D99, D101, D102; none is decided or recommended here)
(a) keep the placeholder rule: no sport passes slot 1 from this venue. (b) exempt exactly 00:00:00Z **per sport**, only where a pre-registered whole-distribution daylight-saving test passes (basketball, hockey on this evidence), which amends D68 and needs its own what-if on resolved events and the schedule. (c) take slot 1 for sports from the schedule endpoint (coverage is low: 8.8 % of the contrast events).
Whichever is chosen, the venue-coverage problem is unchanged: this venue still lists almost none of the esports, culture and politics flow (RESULTS_2026-10-04.md).
